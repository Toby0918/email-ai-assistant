## Changes and causes

ASCII intent keywords now require word boundaries. In the reproduced payment
case, the old `eta` substring matched `details` and selected a shipping workflow.
Standalone boilerplate confidentiality and standard trading terms no longer
replace the current operational request. Address text and order-number fragments
are not promoted to measurements.

Selected PDF extraction now allows 12 pages, 8,000 characters per page and
32,000 characters overall. Existing DOCX/XLSX limits remain 8,000 characters.
Both source text and sanitized text consume the PDF budget; exhausted budgets
stop further page reads and report the limitation. Existing file size, worker,
timeout, decoding and cleanup boundaries remain in place.

Strict document facts retain purchase-row currency, unit price, quantity, net
amount, delivery date and goods/expense role. Decimal arithmetic validates a
recognized row. Finite inspection and shipping fields preserve their object and
source. Unrecognized tables remain outside this narrow parser's capability.

Narrow evidence recognizers project supported receipt/allocation, GRN blocking,
shipping, chronological transfer, purchase revision and scoped quality statements
into consistent conclusions, facts, next steps and review-only drafts. Submitted
visible quoted text can corroborate a receipt or a revision-date comparison;
there is no stored-mail reader. Security flags prevent these projections.
Negated, conditional, corrected and retracted receipts have regression coverage.

## Scope and limitations

This establishes automated acceptance of the eight selected local-rule cases,
not general natural-language understanding or operator business sign-off.
Recognizers support specific bounded text formats; unseen phrasings and arbitrary
table layouts require separate evaluation. PDF text extraction does not establish
the actual selection of graphical checkboxes, signatures, image contents or
unspecified conditional-approval details. Dates and reported receipts still require
business verification before commitments. Remote-provider quality is untested in
this run. No mailbox scanning, sending, deletion or historical import was added.
